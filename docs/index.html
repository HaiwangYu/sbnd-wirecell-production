SBND Wire-Cell Productions

Wire-Cell reconstruction productions for SBND, split by purpose. Training samples run on ALCF Aurora over privately generated MC; evaluation samples run at Fermilab over the official Gen2 SAM definitions. Keeping the two apart reduces the chance of truth leakage between training and evaluation.
